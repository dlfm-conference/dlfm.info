## News

The International Conference on Digital Libraries for Musicology 2027 is planned for Saturday the 11th of September 2027 in London, England. Updates and CFP will be released soon.

## About the conference

The Digital Libraries for Musicology (DLfM) conference presents a venue
specifically for those working on, and with, Digital Library systems and content
in the domain of music and musicology.
